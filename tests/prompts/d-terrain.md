Create an explorable 3D procedural landscape. You may use Three.js r128 loaded from exactly https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js (and nothing else).
- Terrain generated from multi-octave noise written by you (no noise library): mountains, valleys, beaches, with colors by height and slope (sand, grass, rock, snow).
- A water plane with animated waves, reflection-like color and a depth-based shore tint.
- Fog and a sky gradient with a sun; shadows or at least strong directional shading.
- Fly camera: WASD + mouse look (click to capture), keep above the terrain. A seed button regenerates a different world.
- Keep it above 40 fps.
