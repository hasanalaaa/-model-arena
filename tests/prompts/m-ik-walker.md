Create a canvas animation of a procedural creature (a spider or lizard with at least 6 legs, or a 4-legged animal) that walks toward wherever the user clicks.
- Every leg is a 2-bone or 3-bone chain solved with inverse kinematics; no pre-baked animation.
- Feet are planted on the ground while the body moves, then step to a new target when they get too far from the body (alternating gait, never all legs lifting at once).
- The body should bob and rotate to face the direction of travel, and turn smoothly.
- Draw it with nice shapes and shading, a subtle ground texture and a shadow. Without clicks it should wander on its own.
