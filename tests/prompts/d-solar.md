Build an interactive 3D solar system. You may use Three.js r128 loaded from exactly https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js (and nothing else).
- The Sun and 8 planets with orbital speeds and relative sizes that look plausible (not to scale is fine but ordering and relative speeds must be right), Saturn with rings, the Moon orbiting Earth.
- All textures must be generated procedurally in code (canvas textures or shaders): no image files. Earth should show continents and clouds, the Sun should glow.
- Orbit lines, a starfield background, correct lighting from the Sun (planets lit on the side facing the Sun).
- Mouse orbit/zoom camera, click a planet to fly to it and show its name and facts, a time-speed slider.
