Model a recognizable low-poly car (body, cabin with windows, 4 wheels with rims, headlights, taillights, spoiler) from primitive shapes only, and make it drivable on an endless flat ground with a track of cones and road markings. You may use Three.js r128 loaded from exactly https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js (and nothing else).
- Arrow keys/WASD: acceleration, braking, steering with sensible car physics (wheels turn, front wheels steer, body leans and the car drifts a bit at speed).
- Chase camera that follows smoothly. Dynamic shadow under the car, brake lights light up when braking, headlights cast light.
- Cones can be knocked over. Show speed in a HUD.
