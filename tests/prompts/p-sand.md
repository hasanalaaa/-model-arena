Build a falling-sand cellular automaton toy at 200x120 cells or higher, scaled up crisply.
Elements: sand, water, stone (static), fire, wood, oil, plant, and an eraser.
Required interactions: sand sinks in water and oil; oil floats on water; water flows sideways and levels out; fire burns wood and oil and produces smoke and dies out; plants grow upward when touching water; water extinguishes fire.
Paint with the mouse with an adjustable brush size, an element palette, Pause and Clear buttons. It must stay at 60 fps.
