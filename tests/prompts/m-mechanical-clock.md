Draw (SVG or canvas, your choice) a mechanical clock movement seen from the front, showing at least 7 visible gears of different sizes that mesh with each other, plus an escapement (anchor + escape wheel) that ticks.
- The gears must actually mesh: teeth interlock, neighbouring gears turn in opposite directions, and the angular speed ratios are exactly the inverse of the tooth-count ratios.
- A second hand, a minute hand and an hour hand show the real current local time and are driven by the gear train (a 60:1 and 12:1 ratio chain), not by separate timers.
- Add a pendulum or balance wheel that swings in sync with the escapement tick.
- Make it look like polished brass and steel with shading, a dial with numerals, and subtle shadows.
