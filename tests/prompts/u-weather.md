Create a weather app UI with fully animated weather scenes drawn in canvas/SVG/CSS (no images). Using generated data for 5 cities, show for each city: current conditions with a full-screen animated scene (sunny with lens flare and drifting clouds, rain with splashes and lightning flashes, snow with accumulating flakes, fog, night with stars and a moon), a 24-hour temperature curve, a 7-day forecast row, wind direction compass, UV and humidity gauges.
Switching cities cross-fades the scenes. The scene time-of-day matches the city's simulated local time, with a slider to scrub the hour.
