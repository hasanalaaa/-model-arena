Create a soft-body physics toy: several jelly blobs (at least 5, different colors) made of a pressure-based spring-mass model fall into a box with sloped platforms.
- Blobs keep their volume (internal pressure), squash when landing, wobble, and bounce off each other and the walls without tangling or passing through each other.
- You can pick up and throw a blob with the mouse.
- Render each blob as a smooth filled shape with a highlight and a face with eyes that react (squinting on impact).
