Build a 3x3x3 Rubik's-cube-like puzzle in 3D. You may use Three.js r128 loaded from exactly https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js (and nothing else).
- 27 cubies with rounded-looking stickers on the correct 6 colors, and true face rotations animated smoothly (a layer of 9 cubies rotates as a group; cubies keep their identity afterward).
- Buttons: Scramble (20 random moves, animated quickly), Solve (the cube solves itself with animated moves - a simple layer-by-layer solver or by reversing the scramble history is acceptable, but it must end in a truly solved state), Undo.
- Drag to rotate the whole cube; click-drag a face to turn that layer is a bonus.
- Show a move counter and the move list notation (R U R' ...).
